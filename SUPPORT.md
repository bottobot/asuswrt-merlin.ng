# Support

This fork is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Asuswrt-Merlin or ASUS.
Support is limited: one person maintains it in spare time, so replies may be slow or may not
come at all.

**Please do not take anything about this fork to RMerl, the Asuswrt-Merlin issue tracker or the
Merlin forums on SNBForums.** Open an issue in this repository instead, and include the build
commit and the output of `sh /jffs/ap-harden.sh status`.

If a problem also happens on the official Asuswrt-Merlin firmware, with nothing from this fork
installed, report it upstream through Merlin's usual channels.
